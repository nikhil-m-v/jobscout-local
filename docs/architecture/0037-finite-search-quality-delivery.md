## Problem and realistic requirement

Milestone 21 combines software correctness, search-provider coverage, ranking preference, market availability and human acceptance. Repeated extraction fixes cannot make a snippet-only provider disclose facts it did not return. Automated judgments cannot supply human acceptance. Top-ten changes are unavoidable in reranking; the no-supported-primary-eviction rule was the prospective adoption guard for the rejected 21I experiment, not a universal mathematical property all future rankings must satisfy.

The v1.0 outcome is a bounded, private candidate-discovery workflow with same-link evidence, honest uncertainty, reversible filters and inspectable ordering, **plus recorded human usefulness acceptance for its explicitly declared release scope**. Ten–twenty suitable opportunities remains an aspiration when available, never a guaranteed count. Literal support is not personal eligibility, distinct vacancy identity or current availability. Mostly unknown/irrelevant results cannot be passed off as a useful shortlist or proven market scarcity.

Do not promise exhaustive discovery, verified freshness or individual suitability from titles/snippets. Do not require perfect extraction of arbitrary prose, agreement with every automated label, or every supported result simultaneously occupying the first ten. A defect affecting privacy, data loss or unsafe suppression is still blocking. No production page fetching, new search scope, model, dependency, budget or default is authorized by this reset.
